# GSPack 0.8 - Cores and Cosmos

Rebuild your endgame progression and explore a richer Mars.

- Five dedicated Core chapters, an FE crafting multiblock, a five-core Powah Singularity and native 16px artwork.
- Mars gains ice regions, research dungeons, rare loot ruins, oxygen villages with spaceman traders and alien pillager camps.
- Mars-only pillager raids, protected village life support, space-suit zombies and an optional 1% oxygen-module drop.
- A separate 24,660-tick Martian sol, square Phobos and Deimos, distant Mars in the Overworld sky and cave Lumestone.
- 66 Potions Master Ore Sights, stopped Thaumaturge taint spread, seasonal menus and an annual SGPack April Fools logo.
- 43 quest chapters and 1,840 quests; new Mars research feeds the Cosmic Core while the first Moon trip stays accessible.
